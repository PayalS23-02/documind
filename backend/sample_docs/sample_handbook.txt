ATTENDANCE POLICY

A student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to sit for the end-semester examination.

For a backlog course, the minimum attendance requirement is 65%, provided the student has registered for the course in the current semester.

Students falling short of the minimum attendance may apply for condonation through the Dean of Academics, subject to a maximum shortfall of 10%.

GRADING POLICY

Grades are awarded on a 10-point scale. The minimum passing grade in any course is D, corresponding to a grade point of 4.0.

A student who fails to secure a passing grade must reappear for the course as a backlog in a subsequent semester.

REGISTRATION DEADLINES

Course registration for the odd semester closes two weeks before the start of classes.

Late registration is permitted for one additional week with a late fee of Rs. 500 per course.

No registration is permitted after the late registration window closes.
